## 0.1.0

### Added

- ClaimSpec 1.0 line-oriented and JSON contract parsers.
- Fourteen grounding and completion rules.
- Versioned trace, contract, and result JSON Schemas.
- Deterministic evaluator and `init`, `check`, `explain`, and `schema` CLI commands.
- Text, JSON, JUnit XML, and SARIF 2.1.0 reports.
- Twenty-four example contracts, a passing trace, and deliberately failing traces.
- Standard-library `unittest` coverage and a portable CI check script.
